# Hardware Device Overlay

## Authorization-First Work

- Frame firmware, telemetry, protocol, and device-interface work as maintenance
  or interoperability for owned or authorized hardware.
- Ask for authorization and scope if the request could involve unauthorized
  access, evasion, exploitation, or defeating security controls.
- Phrase work in terms of maintenance, recovery, diagnostics, compatibility,
  telemetry analysis, and interoperability.
- Do not provide steps to access third-party systems, defeat security controls,
  extract credentials, bypass licensing, or evade monitoring.

## Evidence

- Prefer observations from the actual device, logs, configuration, or captured
  maintenance data over assumptions.
- Keep device-specific identifiers out of reusable templates and public
  documentation.
- Redact device serial numbers, unique hardware IDs, private network addresses,
  local hostnames, and account-linked identifiers in docs and examples.
